Artifact Overview

This artifact is a program created for CS-300 that loaded course data and sorted it using a BST.

Why I Selected This Artifact

This artifact made the most sense to include as its main purpose was to demonstrate my abilities with algorithms and data structures, which is what this category of the portfolio is also intended to display.

The Enhancement

The enhancement I made was adding an AVL tree as opposed to the BST, which stays balanced maintaining a faster runtime.

Reflection and Challenges

This artifact was difficult in the sense that data structures and pointers were brand new to me at the time, and as more of an abstract concept it was harder for me to grasp
